# Price book — German trades, 2025/26, NET (excl. VAT)

Grounding for the valuation prompt. Verify and extend these before round 1; a wrong
book here is a systematic bias on every single item. Ranges are what a claims expert
would still sign off, not the cheapest possible quote.

## Conventions and red flags

- **19 % USt.** is standard. 7 % appears rarely and almost never on trade work.
- Anfahrt, Entsorgung, Kleinmaterial, Baustellenreinigung are normal line items.
- Gutachterkosten / Sachverständige are often covered separately by the policy — check.
- Typical inflations to catch: doubled quantities; area larger than the damage description
  supports; a whole-room renovation billed for a localised stain; brand-new premium parts
  replacing worn ones; "Sonderzuschlag" with no stated reason; the same work billed twice
  under two descriptions (e.g. both "Estrich entfernen" and "Bodenaufbau Rückbau").
- Betterment (neu für alt) is usually reduced by insurers, not paid in full.

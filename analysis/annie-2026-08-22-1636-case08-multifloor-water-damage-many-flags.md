## What happened

Toilet cistern fill valve failed while family was away. Water ran for hours, came down through guest WC floor into stairwell, then ceiling into living room and hobby room. Robot vacuum dragged water across more parquet. Billiard table soaked (cloth, cues, rails, light). 5 trades on site: Building Services, Dismantling, Drying, Flooring, Plumbing.

**The damage description pre-flags 6 problem areas explicitly** — this is unusual and valuable:
1. Master bedroom redecoration — unaffected room, water never reached it
2. Admin/processing fee
3. Catering for the crew
4. Drying firm billed electricity separately AND added a return visit
5. Two crews each billed waste disposal
6. "Old" cue sets replaced
